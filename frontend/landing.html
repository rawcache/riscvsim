Step Debugger

Clock cycle by cycle with register diff highlighting. Watch x2 change from 0x01 to 0x05 the moment the write-back fires.

Curated assembly challenges with a full test harness. Fix bugs, implement functions, and pass the test suite to unlock the next level.

AI Feedback
